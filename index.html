You Have Been PHISHED!

This was a simulated QR code phishing ("quishing") exercise for Cybersecurity Awareness Week. No harm done, but a malicious QR code could have taken you to a fake login page or downloaded malware to your phone.

Next time, watch for:

  • QR codes from unknown sources, such as random posters, flyers or stickers placed over existing codes
  • QR codes that promise prizes or rewards, or create a sense of urgency

What you could have done:

  • Check the URL preview your camera shows before opening the link, and only continue if it's a trusted, official domain
  • Report suspicious QR codes to the security team instead of scanning them

When in doubt, don't scan. Never enter your credentials on a page you reached through a QR code.
